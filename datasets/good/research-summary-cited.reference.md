# Effect of a structured discharge protocol on 30-day hospital readmissions

## Abstract

A randomised controlled trial enrolled 500 patients at a single urban teaching
hospital between January 2023 and June 2024. Patients were randomised to a
structured discharge protocol or to usual care.

## Findings

The intervention reduced hospital readmissions by 30% over an eighteen-month
follow-up period. The effect was consistent across age groups, with no
significant interaction by age decile.

Secondary outcomes showed a reduction in mean length of stay on readmission,
though this did not reach statistical significance.

## Limitations

The authors note an important limitation: all participants were recruited from a
single urban teaching hospital. The result may not generalise to rural
populations, where discharge support infrastructure differs substantially.

Follow-up was by telephone, which may have under-counted readmissions to other
institutions.

## Funding and registration

The study was funded by a national research council grant. The trial was
registered before enrolment and the protocol is publicly available. Reporting
follows the CONSORT standard for randomised trials.
