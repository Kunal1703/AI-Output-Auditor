# Trial results: readmission reduction

A randomised controlled trial of 500 patients found the new treatment reduced
hospital readmissions by 30% over an eighteen-month follow-up period. The effect
was consistent across age groups.

The authors note an important limitation: all participants were recruited from a
single urban teaching hospital, which limits how far the finding generalises to
rural populations.

Trial registration and protocol are available at
https://clinicaltrials.gov/ and the reporting standard followed is CONSORT
(https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/consort/).

In summary: a 30% reduction in readmissions across 500 patients over eighteen
months, tempered by single-site recruitment that limits generalisability.
