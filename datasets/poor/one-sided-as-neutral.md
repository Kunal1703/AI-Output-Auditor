# Congestion pricing: an overview

Congestion pricing is the obvious answer for any city serious about traffic.
Everyone knows the scheme works: London saw traffic drop and the money went into
transit. The evidence is settled.

Opponents simply dislike change. Some retailers complain about footfall, but
retailers always complain about everything, and there is no serious argument
against the charge on those grounds.

The distributional objection gets raised occasionally by people who have not
looked at the revenue side. Once you account for recycling, it evaporates.

The council should have moved years ago. Every month of delay is a month of
avoidable congestion, and the reasonable position here is not really in dispute
among people who understand the topic.
